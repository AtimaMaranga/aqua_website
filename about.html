Our Story

Creating a Better World, One Community at a Time

Welcome to the Aqua and Agriculture Initiative, a Kenyan Non-Governmental Organization established in 2017 with a mission to create a positive impact in arid and semi-arid regions. Our focus lies in improving water access, promoting agriculture, and supporting educational initiatives in Kitui, Makueni, and Kilifi counties in Kenya.

Aqua A.I believes that collaborative efforts and a long-term perspective can lead to significant impact within a community. Our approach involves direct communication and engagement with the communities we support, allowing us to tailor our programs according to their needs and prioritize what is important to them.

Our goal is to make life better for the people who live in the areas we work in.

What Guides Us

Vision, Mission & Purpose

Our Vision

A food-secure nation where everyone has access to safe and sufficient drinking water.

Our Mission

We strive to improve the quality of life in arid and semi-arid regions of Kenya through mitigating food insecurity and enhancing access to safe and sufficient water.

Our Purpose

To ensure a safe and sufficient water supply to support life and enhance agricultural production in arid and semi-arid regions of Kenya.
